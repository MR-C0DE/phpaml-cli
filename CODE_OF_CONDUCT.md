# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PHPAML contributors, maintainers, and community members are committed to a
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless
of experience, background, identity, disability, appearance, nationality,
religion, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing private communication.
- Prioritize the safety and well-being of the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
trolling, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing private information
without permission are not tolerated. Sustained disruption of community work
or bad-faith participation is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, events,
and other spaces where someone represents PHPAML or its community.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method
listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and as
confidentially as possible. Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant depending
on the nature and history of the behavior.

Anyone responsible for enforcement must disclose conflicts of interest and
step aside when necessary. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern
is prohibited.

## Français

La communauté PHPAML s’engage à offrir un environnement accueillant,
respectueux et sans harcèlement. Les désaccords doivent porter sur les idées et
le code, jamais sur les personnes. Le harcèlement, la discrimination, les
menaces, les attaques personnelles, la divulgation d’informations privées et
les perturbations volontaires ne sont pas tolérés.

Signalez confidentiellement tout problème aux responsables par le moyen de
contact indiqué dans [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Les responsables peuvent
retirer du contenu, avertir une personne, limiter temporairement sa
participation ou l’exclure définitivement. Les représailles sont interdites.
